Install Rocket Book Games

Add the app to your home screen for a full-screen experience with no browser bars.

⚠️ This only works in Safari. If you are using Chrome or another browser on your iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari first.
1
Open Safari
Visit schoolflo.org/rocket_book_games in the Safari browser on your iPhone or iPad.
2
Tap the Share button
On iPhone it is the Share icon at the bottom center of the browser.

On iPad it is the Share icon in the top right of the browser.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share sheet that pops up and tap ➕ Add to Home Screen
4
Confirm the name and tap Add
You can leave the name as-is. Tap Add in the top right corner of the screen.
5
You're all set! 🎉
The Rocket Book Games icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it to open the full-screen app anytime.
ℹ️ These steps are for Chrome. If you are using a different browser the steps may look slightly different, but the option is usually in the browser menu.
1
Open Chrome
Visit schoolflo.org/rocket_book_games in Google Chrome on your Android phone or tablet.
2
Open the browser menu
Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner of Chrome.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Look for Add to Home Screen or Install App in the menu. Either one works.
4
Confirm
Tap Add or Install on the prompt that appears.
5
You're all set! 🎉
The Rocket Book Games icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it to open the full-screen app anytime.
